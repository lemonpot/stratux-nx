An independent distribution based on the open-source Stratux project, adapted and maintained by Lemonpot.

Where Stratux began

Stratux was founded in 2015 by pilot and programmer Chris Young (@cyoung) with other open-source contributors. Their goal was an affordable, modular ADS-B weather and traffic receiver built around Raspberry Pi.

The community expanded the project with dual-band reception, GPS, AHRS and broader regional support. Since 2020, b3nn0 and contributors have continued maintaining and extending the upstream codebase.

What Stratux NX adds

Stratux NX starts with the open-source Stratux project. Lemonpot adapts, integrates and extends that foundation to make the receiver simpler to operate and easier to maintain. NX adds a redesigned interface, online updates, Internet usage controls, automatic flight history, cloud synchronization and device-focused workflows.

We continue developing Stratux NX as a distinct distribution while following and crediting the upstream open-source project.
