Stratux with ForeFlight, Garmin Pilot, SkyDemon and other EFB apps

Stratux and Stratux NX send traffic, weather, GPS position and attitude over GDL90, the format most EFB apps read. You join the Stratux Wi-Fi network and pick it in your app. There's nothing to pair and no account to create.

Garmin Pilot on Android does not support Stratux. Attitude needs an AHRS board in the Stratux, and it is a backup aid only.

The basics, for any app

  1. Turn on the Stratux and wait until its Wi-Fi network appears.
  2. On the tablet, join the Stratux network. The tablet may say it has no Internet; that's expected unless you set up Internet sharing.
  3. Open your app and select Stratux, a "GDL90 device" or an "external ADS-B" source.
  4. Check http://192.168.10.1 if something seems wrong: the status page shows the radios, GPS and traffic count.
Use one tablet as the primary EFB and keep others on the same Stratux network. Stratux sends to every connected device.

ForeFlight

  1. Join the Stratux Wi-Fi.
  2. Open ForeFlight. It finds Stratux on its own. Check More → Devices to see the connection, GPS and traffic status.
  • With Hide Distant Traffic on, ForeFlight hides traffic more than 15 NM away or more than 3,500 ft above or below you.
  • If traffic altitudes look wrong, check the ownship settings in the Stratux settings page.
  • Synthetic vision terrain works without AHRS. Pitch and roll need an AHRS board.

Garmin Pilot (iPad and iPhone)

  1. Join the Stratux Wi-Fi.
  2. In Garmin Pilot, open the Devices settings and select Stratux.

Garmin Pilot shows Stratux traffic and weather on iOS. It does not use the Stratux attitude data, and the Android version doesn't support Stratux.

SkyDemon

SkyDemon can read Stratux in two ways.

GDL90

  1. Settings → Third-Party Devices → enable GDL90 Compatible Device.
  2. When you start Go Flying, choose GDL90 Compatible Device.

FLARM (NMEA)

Stratux also offers FLARM-style NMEA on TCP port 2000, which SkyDemon can use through its FLARM device options. The Stratux community recommends this for traffic without position (Mode S targets with no bearing). See the Stratux wiki for the exact SkyDemon settings.

FltPlan Go, Avare, EasyVFR and others

  • FltPlan Go: join the Stratux Wi-Fi and select Stratux as the ADS-B device in the app's settings.
  • Avare: join the Stratux Wi-Fi, then enable the external ADS-B or GDL90 option in Avare's settings.
  • EasyVFR: Settings → GDL90 Settings → Use GDL90. You can also use the GDL90 GPS as the app's GPS.
  • Any other app: search its settings for "Stratux", "GDL90" or "external ADS-B". Any of those means it should work.
