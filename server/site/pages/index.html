Free, open-source software for your Stratux.

Stratux NX is a Stratux image for Raspberry Pi. It sends ADS-B traffic and weather to your EFB like Stratux always has, and adds an automatic flight log with block and air time, data limits for Starlink, and online updates.

What it does

Everything Stratux does, plus a few things pilots kept asking for.

Stratux NX is built on the open-source stratux/stratux code. Traffic, weather, GPS and AHRS work the same way. The additions are below.

Automatic flight log

Each flight is detected and timed on its own: off-block, takeoff, landing and on-block, with the route, altitude, speed and touch-and-goes. How it works

Data limits for Starlink

See which tablet, phone or laptop is using your onboard Internet, set a limit for each one, and pause a device when it reaches it. Starlink guide

Keeps flights after a power cut

Flights are written to the SD card while they happen. If power is cut, the flight is recovered on the next boot and marked as incomplete.

Online updates

Install new versions from the Settings page when the Stratux has Internet, or upload the update file when it doesn't. Settings and flights are kept.

Optional cloud

Link your Stratux to a free account to replay flights in 3D and share them with a link. Nothing is visible until you link it.

Weather saved with the flight

FIS-B METARs, TAFs and NEXRAD received during the flight are stored with it, so you can see later what you had on board.

Offline airports and time zones

Departure and arrival airports and local times are worked out on the device, without Internet.

A clearer web interface

A redesigned status, settings and map interface that works on phones, tablets and laptops, with plain-language health checks.

Also included: Adaptive AHRS v2 (beta). It is experimental, so always cross-check attitude with your aircraft instruments.

Starlink

Make a 50 GB plan last.

Starlink's aviation plans for the Mini include 50 GB a month. One phone update or photo backup can use several of those in a single flight. Stratux NX connects to the Starlink Wi-Fi and shares it with your devices, so it can measure and limit each one.

  • Usage per device, live and for the whole session.
  • A warning, then a pause at the limits you choose, plus a cap for the session.
  • No limit for the pilot's iPad if you want. A paused device stays connected to Stratux, so traffic and weather keep reaching its EFB.
  • A list of which apps used the data: software updates, photo backup, video and so on.

Optional cloud

Replay and share flights with a free account.

When your Stratux has Internet, it uploads finished flights. They stay private until you enter the receiver's link code at app.stratuxnx.com. You don't need the cloud to use Stratux NX.

app.stratuxnx.com · shared flight

3D replay

Follow the flight in time and altitude, with the traffic your Stratux received.

Vertical profile and landing view

Climb, cruise, descent and a GPS-based look at each touchdown.

Share with a link

Send a flight to family, a student or your instructor. They don't need an account.

Several receivers, one account

Each Stratux has a permanent link code. Only your account sees its flights.

Download the SD card image

The .zip from the latest release. No need to unzip it.

Write it to a microSD card

With Raspberry Pi Imager (Choose OS → Use Custom) or balenaEtcher. Don't apply OS customisation.

Boot and connect

Join the Stratux Wi-Fi network, open http://192.168.10.1, then open your EFB app. App setup

Optional: Internet and cloud

Settings → WiFi Mode → AP+Client to join Starlink or a hotspot, then set your data limits.

Open source

Built on Stratux, given back as open source.

Stratux NX is an independent distribution of the community-maintained stratux/stratux project, which continues the original work by Christopher Young and many contributors. Our changes are published under the BSD 3-Clause license.

There is nothing to buy. Every release includes the exact source used to build it. Bug reports and pull requests are welcome.
