Stratux vs Sentry vs Stratus, and Stratux NX vs Stratux

Portable ADS-B receivers all do the same core job: traffic, weather and GPS for your tablet. They differ in price, which apps they work with, battery and extras. Here is a factual comparison, followed by what Stratux NX changes compared with standard Stratux.

How to choose

  • You only use ForeFlight and want a sealed box with a long battery and a CO alarm: Sentry is built for that.
  • You use Garmin Pilot and want a Garmin-native device: the GDL 50 connects over Bluetooth.
  • You want a screen on the receiver and a replaceable battery: Stratus 4.
  • You want the lowest cost, any app, repairable parts, and you don't mind a bigger box and an external battery: Stratux. It is also the only option where you can change the software.

Stratux NX vs standard Stratux

Stratux NX is a distribution of the open-source stratux/stratux project. Both are free and run on the same hardware. The receiver itself works the same way. The differences are in what's built around it.

If standard Stratux already does everything you need, there's no reason to switch. If you want an automatic logbook record of every flight or you fly with Starlink, Stratux NX may be useful. Switching back is just a matter of putting your old card back in.
