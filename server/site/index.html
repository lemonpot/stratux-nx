Everything in one box

All of Stratux. Plus the parts you always wished it had.

Stratux NX keeps the proven open-source receiver underneath and adds the workflows pilots actually use every day, on the ground and in the air.

Automatic flight log

Every flight is detected, timed and stored on its own: off-block, takeoff, landing and on-block, plus route, altitude, speed and touch-and-goes.

Starlink data protection

See which iPad, phone or laptop is using your onboard Internet, set limits per device and for the whole session, and pause the heavy users automatically.

Cloud sync & sharing

Flights upload by themselves whenever there is Internet. Replay them in 3D and share a link with anyone, even if they don't have an account.

Power-loss proof

Flights are written to physical storage as they happen. Pull the master switch and the flight is recovered on the next boot, using its own recorded times.

Online updates

Update from the Stratux web page in one tap. Each update keeps your settings and flights. No Internet? Upload the update file instead.

NX Guardian

A single health check for radios, GPS, storage and system state, with clear next steps when something needs attention before you depart.

Weather journey

FIS-B METARs, TAFs and NEXRAD received in flight are saved with the flight, so you can see what the weather really was along your route.

Landing signature

A GPS view of the final approach and touchdown: groundspeed, descent and timing. Ghost Landing compares it with your previous arrivals.

Offline airports & time zones

84,512 airports and a full time zone database on board, so departure, arrival and local times work with no Internet at all.

Adaptive AHRS v2 beta

A new attitude estimator that adapts to changing flight conditions. Experimental: always cross-check with your instruments.

A modern interface

A redesigned, responsive web app for phone, tablet and laptop, with light and dark themes and plain-language status everywhere.

Starlink, under control

Don't burn your 50 GB plan in one flight.

Bringing Starlink into the cockpit is a game changer, until a passenger's phone starts an iOS update or a laptop syncs its photo library at 9,000 feet. Stratux NX sits between your devices and the dish and keeps every megabyte accountable.

  • Live usage per device. See exactly who is using data right now and how much they have used this session.
  • Warn, then pause. Get a warning at one amount, pause that device at another, and cap the total for the session.
  • Protect what matters. Exempt the pilot's iPad. Local traffic to Stratux NX and ForeFlight is never blocked.
  • Know where it went. A ranking of the apps and services that used the most data this session.
  • Any uplink. Starlink Mini, a phone hotspot or an LTE router: Stratux NX joins it as a client and shares it safely.

Automatic flight log

From the first turn of the prop to the last.

Turn on the avionics and forget about it. Stratux NX detects movement, takeoff, landing and parking from sustained GPS speed, climb and nearby-airport context, then writes a complete record with block time and air time. No buttons, no paperwork.

  • Starts by itself. A few seconds above taxi speed opens a new flight. Short GPS spikes are ignored.
  • Closes by itself. 45 seconds stopped after landing closes the flight and sets the on-block time.
  • Logbook-ready times. Off-block, takeoff, landing and on-block in UTC/Zulu, shown in your local time zone. Touch-and-goes counted.
  • Every second, on disk. The full route, altitude, groundspeed and vertical speed, plus the weather and traffic context of the flight.
  • Survives power loss. Unfinished flights are recovered at boot. If power was lost in the air, the flight is kept and clearly marked as incomplete.

Flash it, boot it, go fly.

Get the hardware

  • Raspberry Pi 4 (recommended), 3B+ or 5, with a good 5 V / 3 A power supply
  • microSD card, 16 GB or larger, from a reputable brand
  • SDR radios: 1090 MHz for ADS-B traffic everywhere, plus 978 MHz for UAT traffic and FIS-B weather in the US
  • GPS receiver (USB). Optional: an AHRS sensor board for attitude
Already have a Stratux? Stratux NX runs on the same hardware. Keep your old card as a backup and flash a new one.

Download the image

Download the SD card image (.zip) from the latest release. There's no need to unzip it, the flashing tools read it directly.

Download SD card image

Flash the microSD card

With Raspberry Pi Imager (Windows, macOS, Linux):

  1. Choose Device: your Raspberry Pi model
  2. Choose OS → scroll down → Use Custom → select the downloaded .zip
  3. Choose Storage: your microSD card
  4. Click Next, answer No to OS customisation, then Yes to write

Prefer balenaEtcher? Choose Flash from file, select the .zip, select the card, then Flash!

Don't apply OS customisation (Wi-Fi, user or hostname settings) in Raspberry Pi Imager. Stratux NX configures itself.

Boot and connect

Insert the card, connect the radios and GPS, and power on. The first boot takes a couple of minutes.

  • On your tablet or phone, join the Wi-Fi network Stratux
  • Open http://192.168.10.1 to see the Stratux NX dashboard
  • Open your EFB app. ForeFlight, Garmin Pilot, SkyDemon and other GDL90 apps detect Stratux automatically

Add Internet and your account (optional)

  • In Settings, set WiFi Mode to AP+Client and join your Starlink, hotspot or router network (2.4 GHz)
  • In Internet Data, set your warning, per-device and session limits
  • In Flight Log, note the link code, then enter it at app.stratuxnx.com from any device with Internet
Already running Stratux NX? Open Settings → Software Update and install the latest version online, or upload the .deb file if you're offline.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Is Stratux NX certified avionics?

No. Like Stratux, it provides supplemental situational awareness only. It must never be your sole source of navigation, traffic, weather, attitude or terrain information. Adaptive AHRS v2 is a beta feature: always cross-check it with your aircraft instruments.

Do I need Internet in the aircraft?

No. Traffic, weather, the flight log and the airport database all work completely offline. Internet is only used for cloud sync, online updates and map tiles, and Stratux NX helps you control how much of it is used.

Which EFB apps work with it?

Any app that supports GDL90 over Wi-Fi, including ForeFlight, Garmin Pilot, SkyDemon, FltPlan Go and Avare. Connect your tablet to the Stratux Wi-Fi and the app finds it automatically.

Who can see my flights?

Only you. When your Stratux has Internet it uploads its flights privately to Stratux NX Cloud. They stay invisible until you link the receiver to your account with its code, and then they appear only in your account. You decide which flights to share with a link.

Does it work outside the United States?

Yes. 1090 MHz ADS-B traffic, GPS, the flight log and Starlink controls work worldwide, and the airport database is global. 978 MHz UAT and FIS-B weather are only broadcast in the United States.

How much does it cost?

Stratux NX is free and open source. You only need the hardware, which is the same as a regular Stratux build.

How do I update?

From Settings → Software Update on your Stratux. With Internet it downloads and installs the latest release in a few minutes, keeping your settings and flight history. Without Internet you can upload the update file downloaded from GitHub.
